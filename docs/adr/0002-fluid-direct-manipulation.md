# ADR 0002: Fluid direct manipulation

Status: Accepted

Point supplies continuous fingertip focus without activation. A speed-adaptive filter settles camera noise while preserving deliberate movement. Four stable point samples arm selection. A short forward index stroke, measured as fingertip depth relative to the palm and normalised by palm size, freezes targeting at its first intentional movement and commits against that locked tile. Lateral movement cancels a forward-tap candidate and incomplete strokes time out, preventing a card from remaining stuck. Thumb-index proximity no longer activates media. Point-and-forward-tap directly activates modal actions, while closing a deliberately shown open palm into a stabilized fist dismisses the details modal; a fist pose alone cannot close it. An open palm supplies continuous catalog panning in two dimensions. Palm navigation uses a stable palm-centre landmark and updates a target on every tracked sample; render-frame exponential following fills the gaps between inference samples, and measured velocity provides an interruptible non-oscillating release. A stable fist on the catalog launches one rapid momentum shuffle and cannot repeat until re-armed by opening the hand. Up to three missed detector frames retain the active pose without inventing movement. While the wall is moving or settling—or while a visible hand is not in the point pose—targeting, focus feedback, and selection arming are suspended. Mouse and touch provide equivalent input. Motion remains interruptible and input latency takes priority over visual quality.
